About FundLink

Our Mission

FundLink revolutionizes humanitarian donations by leveraging blockchain technology to ensure complete transparency, instant transfers, and maximum impact for your charitable giving.

Built on the Avalanche Fuji testnet, FundLink provides a secure, fast, and transparent platform where donors can support verified NGOs with cryptocurrency donations while tracking the real-world impact of their contributions.

How It Works

Verified NGOs

All NGOs undergo a thorough verification process to ensure legitimacy and proper registration for humanitarian work.

Crypto Donations

Support causes using AVAX or USDT on the Avalanche network for fast, low-cost transactions.

Impact Tracking

Follow your donations from transaction to impact with regular updates from NGOs on their progress and achievements.

Full Transparency

Every donation is recorded on the blockchain, making all transactions publicly verifiable and transparent.
